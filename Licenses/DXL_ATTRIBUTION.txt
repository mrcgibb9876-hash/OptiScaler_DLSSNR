DXL (DLSS eXtended Loader)
==========================

Project:  DXL by LCPD15
Source:   https://github.com/LCPD15/DXL
Licence:  AGPL-3.0

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The Present placement of DLSS 5 Neural Rendering follows DXL's approach. The code in this
project is its own, written against OptiScaler's pass, but these designs come from DXL:

  * Running the Neural Rendering pass at the game's Present, on a command list of its own,
    for games whose swapchain OptiScaler does not wrap (OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_PresentRoute.cpp).

  * Hooking Present and Present1 by detouring the functions a throwaway swapchain reveals, rather
    than writing the shared vtable entry (DXL's PresentInlineHooks.h), and learning the frame's
    command queue from ExecuteCommandLists.

  * Finding the game's scene depth by watching its depth buffers -- DSV creation, render target
    binds, depth clears and resource barriers -- and scoring the candidates by render size, bind
    count and shadow-map shape, keeping the previous choice on a tie
    (OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_DepthTracker.cpp, adapted from DXL's src/core/DepthTracker.cpp).

  * Motion vectors from optical flow for games that give none: AMD FidelityFX Optical Flow run on
    the finished frames and densified to a per-pixel field (OptiScaler_OpticalFlow.dll, built from
    OptiScaler/dlssnr/opticalflow, adapted from DXL's src/core/OpticalFlow.cpp). The FidelityFX
    subset, its precompiled kernels and the densify kernel are the ones DXL ships; FidelityFX is
    AMD's, under the MIT licence in Licenses/FidelityFX_OpticalFlow_LICENSE.txt.

Where this project departs from DXL: the DSV handle table is a hash table rather than a 128-entry
list, candidates are not dropped after 240 idle frames, descriptor copies carry their DSV mapping,
a letterboxed picture is processed as a rectangle inside the frame, and the optical-flow downsample
is a shader of this project's own that stretches the picture per axis to match the densify kernel.

Thank you to LCPD15 for publishing DXL's source.
